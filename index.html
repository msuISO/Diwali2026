दीपावली

Dīpāvalī, from dīpa, a lamp, and āvali, a row.

Diwali is the festival of lights, celebrated by Hindus, Sikhs, Jains, and some Buddhists. It falls on the new moon, the darkest night of the month, so homes are lit with rows of lamps. People draw rangoli patterns in colored powder at their doors, share sweets, and spend the evening with family and friends.

You don’t need to have celebrated Diwali before to come.

Hosted by the Indian Students’ Organisation at Michigan State University.
